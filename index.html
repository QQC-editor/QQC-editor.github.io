Sunny Days & Seafood Paella in Barcelona 01.10.2025

My first day in Barcelona was filled with golden sunshine and the crisp breeze from the Mediterranean Sea. Walking down the famous La Rambla, I was immediately drawn into the vibrant energy of the city. We spent the afternoon admiring Gaudi’s incredible architecture at Park Güell the colorful mosaic tiles against the clear blue sky were breathtaking!
Of course, no trip to Spain is complete without food. For dinner, we sat at a small seaside bistro and ordered a hot pan of authentic Seafood Paella, accompanied by a glass of ice-cold sangria. The rich flavors of saffron, fresh seafood, and perfectly cooked rice were absolutely unforgettable!

Cobblestones and Coffee: A Rainy Afternoon in Paris 04.04.2024

Paris welcomed us with a gentle autumn rain, turning the cobbled streets of Le Marais into shiny, reflective paths. Instead of rushing to shelter, we took a slow stroll along the Seine, watching the iconic silhouette of Notre-Dame emerge through the misty drizzle. There is something endlessly romantic about Paris when it rains—the whole city feels like a quiet, vintage classic film. To escape the cold, we ducked into a charming corner bistro filled with the golden glow of yellow lamps and the rich scent of fresh espresso. We spent hours chatting over buttery croissants and warm café au lait, watching locals pass by outside. Sitting there with good company and watching the city move at its own peaceful pace was easily the highlight of the trip.

Into the Mist: Exploring the Wild Green Heart of Guizhou 07.09.2023

September in Guizhou felt like stepping into an ancient ink wash painting. The air was cool, fresh, and filled with the soothing sounds of cascading water as we traveled through its lush green mountains. Standing in front of the majestic Huangguoshu Waterfall, feeling the giant mist on my face, was completely awe-inspiring. The dramatic karst landscape and hidden valleys made every turn on the road feel like discovering a secret world. Beyond the stunning nature, the highlight was wandering through the Miao and Dong minority villages tucked into the hills. We spent quiet evenings listening to local folk songs, admiring intricate silver craftsmanship, and sharing hot steaming bowls of sour fish soup (酸汤鱼). Guizhou’s authentic charm, breathtaking views, and rich flavor truly made this journey one for the soul.
